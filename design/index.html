Three directions for the new UI

The current site works, but it looks like a generic analytics dashboard: SaaS teal, KPI tiles, every block a shadowed card, emoji as icons, and three rows of filters before any content. A city app should feel like it belongs to the neighbourhood. Below are three working prototypes using the real archive (last 7 days). Each is clickable: open a report, filter, search. They are in Romanian, like the final app.

Option A

Gazeta Sesizărilor

The neighbourhood newspaper. Calm, credible, made to be read.

Ideas

  • Numbers told as a sentence: “Una din trei a fost rezolvată”
  • Reports become short news items with a readable headline
  • Status as an ink stamp, the city's reply as a pull quote
  • Newspaper sections instead of a filter bar; neighbourhood picked inside a sentence
  • Side column: map of the week, “places that keep coming back”, most-used reply

Best for

  • Older residents, local press, sharing on Facebook groups; trust and transparency

Trade-offs

  • Map is secondary; less suited to “what's on my street right now”
  • Serif headlines need care on very small screens

Option B

Harta Cartierului

Map first, like a transit or navigation app. Fast, practical, local.

Ideas

  • Full-screen map; the list shows only what's visible, sorted by distance once you share your location
  • “Caută strada ta”: real street search (OpenStreetMap), plus ticket numbers
  • Big category buttons with icons; pins show the category icon, ring colour shows the outcome
  • Phone: draggable bottom sheet (peek / half / full). Desktop: side panel
  • Report view with a delivery-style tracker: Trimisă → În lucru → Rezolvată
  • Street-sign blue, a typeface designed for low vision, dark mode

Best for

  • “What's happening on my street?”, people out walking, quick checks on the phone

Trade-offs

  • Statistics and trends are hidden; would need a separate “Statistici” screen
  • Depends on the map loading well on weak connections

Option C

Vecinii din S3

Warm and conversational. Feels like a neighbourhood group chat, not a government form.

Ideas

  • Each report is a short conversation: the neighbour's message, then the city hall's reply
  • No reply yet? A “typing…” bubble and “așteaptă de 3 ore”, which makes waiting visible
  • Filters as one sentence: “Arată-mi … despre … din …” (no filter jargon)
  • Progress rings, “Locul săptămânii” card with a mini map
  • Report view as a thread, with status changes as small system messages

Best for

  • People who don't like maps or dashboards; younger users; making the city hall's responsiveness tangible

Trade-offs

  • Takes more scrolling per report; fewer reports on screen
  • Playful tone may feel less “official”

What all three have in common (and the current site lacks)

  • Plain-language statuses: “Rezolvată”, “În lucru”, “Închisă fără rezolvare” instead of “Rezolvată Nefavorabil”
  • 9 friendly topic groups instead of 29 city categories (e.g. all parking types → “Parcare”)
  • A custom drawn icon set, no emoji
  • Readable titles generated from each description (greetings removed, masked plates kept masked)
  • Big tap targets (44 px+), body text 17 px+, colour never the only signal (labels + icons)
  • Mobile-first; works on desktop too
  • Clearly marked as unofficial, with no city hall logo or branding, so it can't be mistaken for the official app
  • “Semnalează aici” sends people to the official form at the same spot

Prototypes: only the main screens are built, for the last 7 days and in Romanian. The full feature set of the current site (history, Insights, export, RSS, English, installable app) would move into whichever direction you choose. Directions can also be mixed, e.g. B's map with C's conversation view.
